## Open questions

- Where the per-SKU stock location data lives in production is not decided; 7.5 lists a scheduled import into OrderCore, holding it in Billing, or a stock-location service, and the HEL-142 high-level design assumes an import that has not been approved.
- Where the carrier cut-off value lives is not decided; it is held in the warehouse operations spreadsheet today.
- The Appendix B bank holiday list is a pasted image, not data, so the working-day part of the calculation has no usable source yet.
- Section 7.2 gives no tie-break for a cart split evenly across Leeds and Bristol, where neither warehouse holds a majority of lines.
- The delivery postcode is held on the customer account, not the order (7.2); the behaviour for a guest cart or a customer who has not signed in is not stated.
- 7.4 says the reason the window could not be calculated is recorded for Insight, but does not say which reason codes exist or where they are stored.
- Neither the ticket nor section 7 states how the window behaves for a cart that is empty.
- "16:00 local time" (7.2) is not defined against a specific timezone or against the behaviour on clock-change days.
- 7.3 says the window shown at confirmation is the one recorded against the order, but neither the ticket nor section 7 says what a tester should see if the recorded window later becomes stale.
